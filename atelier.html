The Maison Manifesto

We reject seasonal obsolescence in pursuit of architectural permanence.

WARDROBE was founded in response to the frantic acceleration of fashion. Rather than generating twenty-four trend cycles each year, we operate as a slow couture workshop—polishing sixteen definitive forms that serve as the foundation of an enduring life.

Pillar I

100% Traceable Natural Fibers

Synthetic polyester and acrylic blends have no place in our collection. Every single yard of cloth is documented from its agricultural origin: Grade-A pasture-sheared Mongolian cashmere, RWS-certified non-mulesed merino, and certified organic Egyptian long-staple cotton.

Our wool is spun in Biella, Italy—a sub-Alpine valley famed for the mineral purity of its mountain meltwater, which softens raw fleece without the application of harsh chemical surfactants.

01

Zero-Waste Cutting

By designing pattern pieces using modular geometric nesting formulas, our cutters achieve a 96% fabric yield—virtually eliminating the cutting-room scraps that plague mass production. Leftover selvages are repurposed for pocket stays and internal binding.

02

Seamless 3D Circular Knitting

Our knitwear is crafted in Hawick, Scotland, utilizing WholeGarment circular looms. Garments are rendered three-dimensionally in a continuous loop of yarn, eliminating side seams, shoulder seams, and armhole bulk for absolute freedom of movement.

03

Lifetime Repair Pledge

We consider our garments lifelong commitments. Should a hem unravel, a button loosen, or a seam need re-pressing, our ateliers in Paris, Tokyo, and New York provide complimentary restoration services for the lifespan of the piece.
